In today's fast-paced world, engineering teams must delve into their deployment pipelines to find inefficiencies before those inefficiencies find them. It's important to note that most outages trace back to release processes rather than to the code itself, and teams that leverage continuous delivery see the difference within a quarter.

Adopting trunk-based development has been a game-changer for many organizations because it removes the merge conflicts that long-lived branches create. The transition isn't just a tooling change, since it demands a rich tapestry of cultural adjustments across the whole team, and the payoff is a seamless path from commit to production.

In conclusion, at the end of the day the work is not just about shipping faster but about building the confidence to ship at all, and that confidence comes from practice rather than from purchasing another platform.
